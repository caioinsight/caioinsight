# Readiness and AEO pivot, validation kit

## The bet (framed)
As AI becomes a buying interface, baby and kids DTC brands need their product data structured and machine-actionable so AI agents can correctly find, represent, and transact their products. Cowy would audit and fix that as a done-for-you service. The open question is whether this is a painful, payable problem today, or too early.

## Pre-committed kill number (set before any calls)
Run 8 interviews with baby and kids DTC operators. A call counts as a YES only if the person names AI-agent representation or transactability as a real problem within the next 6 months AND says they would pay to address it now.
- KILL the pivot if fewer than 3 of 8 are a YES.
- 3 to 4 of 8 is marginal: run one small paid pilot before committing.
- 5 or more of 8: real demand, design the offer.

## Who to talk to
Founders or heads of ecommerce or growth at baby and kids DTC brands. The Tier 1 and Tier 2 CRM list is the pool. Prefer Shopify brands, since that is the niche.

## Interview script (about 15 minutes, listen more than you talk, do not pitch)
Open: "I am researching how AI is changing the way parents find and buy products. Five quick questions, and there is no pitch at the end."
1. When a parent uses AI to research or shop in your category, what do you think happens for your brand today?
2. Have you seen any sales or traffic from AI tools or AI shopping agents yet, and roughly how much?
3. Do you know whether an AI agent can correctly find your products, with the right size, price, and stock, and complete a purchase?
4. Who owns keeping your product data right across these surfaces today, and how much time does it take?
5. If someone audited what AI agents get wrong about buying your products and fixed it, what would that be worth, and would you want it now or in a year?
Close: "Who else should I talk to?"

## What counts as a real signal, not politeness
Real: they name the problem before you describe it, they can name money or time it costs them, or they ask when they can have it.
Not real: "interesting," "makes sense," "send me info." Vague enthusiasm is a no.

## After the calls
Tally the YES count against the kill number and honor it. Log the decision and the best quotes in PIVOT_LOG.md. If KILL, write the wind-down note and stop. If PASS, design the smallest paid pilot, one brand and one fixed-fee audit, before any platform build.
