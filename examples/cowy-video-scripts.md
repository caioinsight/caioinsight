# Cowy 60-Second Video Scripts

Six scripts: 1 brand overview, 1 portfolio overview, 4 product deep-dives. Each ~150 words VO ≈ 60s. Format: **[VISUAL]** / VO.

> Product scripts (3–6) use **[capture live]** for the real AI answer. NEVER invent it. Run the actual query, screen-record it, then fill. These scripts are the wrapper around real footage, not a substitute.

## Production notes
- Each VO ≈ 150 words = natural 60s pace. If delivery is slower, trim the middle line rather than rush.
- Product scripts (3–6) require the live AI query captured first (Path B / Audit Runner). Never fill [capture live] from memory.
- Captions are written for silent autoplay on social (single line, hook-first). Pair with on-screen text of the same line.
- Brand voice: plain, parent-aware, no hype, no repetition.
