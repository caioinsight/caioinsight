## Notes / honest flags

- **Bobbie is a hard first target** (PE-backed, likely has a marketing team). Use this as your method-proving rep. The identical teardown works on smaller funded brands (Little Spoon, Mushie, newer formula entrants) where the founder is more reachable and the yes is faster.
- **Fill the brackets truthfully.** A generic version with placeholders reads like every other cold pitch and kills the advantage. The real screenshot is the whole point.
- **Verify the wrong-fact claim yourself** before stating it in writing to a brand. Run the query, confirm the error is actually showing, then cite it. Never assert an inaccuracy you haven't personally seen on screen.
- **Tone stays calm and specific**, never alarmist. You're a helpful observer showing them something true, not a salesperson.
