## What this means for the business
1. Lead with monitoring (AI Watchdog). The value for accurate brands is certainty plus catching the drift when it happens, not "AI is getting you wrong," because mostly it is not.
2. The audit is most likely to find real errors on: newer or thinly-documented brands, brands with complex or frequently-changing catalogs, and at moments of change (recall, reformulation, new launch). Perplexity and ChatGPT drift more than Google.
3. Re-verify every finding the day it ships. Two of the Tubby Todd findings decayed within a day.
