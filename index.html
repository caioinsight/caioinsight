Why it matters

AI does not go looking for you. It names what it already trusts.

When someone asks AI about your product, it answers from whatever it has read about you, accurate or not. If the sources are thin, old, or contradictory, AI fills the gap with a wrong answer. Four things decide whether it gets you right.

Labels AI can read

Behind every page is hidden text that tells AI what you sell, what it costs, and how people rate it. When it is missing or wrong, AI has to guess, and usually picks someone else.

Reviews and ratings

AI leans on reviews to decide who is worth naming. If your reviews are few or old, you look unproven, and you drop off the list.

Mentions on other sites

AI trusts you more when other places talk about you. Think Reddit, review sites, and articles. If only your own site mentions you, AI treats you as a stranger.

Rules and accuracy

AI can say the wrong thing about you, or suggest you in a place where you are not allowed to sell. Most checkups miss this. For some products, it really matters.

Made for safety-rated gear

Getting into AI is now automatic. Getting the facts right is not.

Shopify now feeds your products into ChatGPT and Google on its own. That gets you listed. It does not make AI pick you over a competitor, and it does not catch when AI states a wrong fact about what you sell. That gap is the work.

The risk nobody checks
An AI tool can tell a parent the wrong fit, the wrong compatibility, or an out-of-date safety claim about your product. That is a lost sale or a costly return. Sometimes worse: a parent who no longer trusts you. Cowy checks for this on every report, because for your kind of product it is not optional.

Straight answers

Questions store owners actually ask.

How do I get my store to show up in ChatGPT?

Make your store easy for AI to read and trust. That means clear labels on your products and prices, steady reviews, and mentions on other sites AI reads. AI names sources it can read and check. Cowy's report shows exactly which of these you are missing.

Why does AI name my competitors and not me?

Because it can read and trust them, and not you yet. Maybe your labels are missing. Maybe your reviews are thin. Maybe no other site mentions you. Any of these drops you off the list, however good your product or ads are. The report points to the exact reason.

How is this different from regular SEO?

SEO got you onto Google's list of links. This gets you named inside the answer that ChatGPT or Perplexity writes back. It runs on clear labels, strong reviews, and who vouches for you. Not just keywords.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. The report is written in plain words, with the fixes ranked by what matters most. If you have a developer, the report hands them the exact steps and code. If you do not, the monthly plan lets us do all of it for you.

I sell car seats or strollers. Does fit and safety accuracy matter here?

More than almost anywhere. Parents ask AI which products fit together and which are safest. A wrong answer means a lost sale or a costly return. Sometimes a trust problem you do not want. Every Cowy report checks whether AI states your fit, compatibility, and safety details correctly. Most checkups never look at this.

What do the monthly fixes actually involve?

A mix of writing and light technical work. Clearer specs and compatibility details. Stronger reviews. Correct safety and fit labels AI can read. Mentions on the parenting sites AI trusts. We handle it, show you what changed, and prove the lift each month.

FAQ

AI accuracy for baby & kids brands, answered

Is AI giving parents wrong facts about my product?

Often, yes. AI assistants answer from whatever they have read, and product data online is frequently thin, outdated, or contradictory. The only way to know is to ask AI the questions parents ask and read the answers. Cowy checks what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini say about your baby brand and flags every wrong, outdated, or misattributed fact.

How do I fix wrong information AI is showing about my brand?

You fix the sources AI reads. That means clean, consistent structured data and factual claims on your site, and correcting the third-party pages AI trusts. Cowy traces each wrong answer to its source, fixes what you control, out-publishes what you don't, then re-checks and proves the correction.

What happens if AI states a wrong ingredient or age range for my product?

On a kids' product that's serious: it can cost the sale and, worse, mislead a parent on a safety-relevant detail. Courts have already held a company liable for a wrong answer its own chatbot gave. Cowy finds those wrong answers and corrects the sources behind them before they do damage.

How do I keep AI accurate about my brand over time?

AI answers drift as sources change month to month, so a one-time fix doesn't hold. Cowy's Watchdog monitors ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini for your brand and alerts you the moment one states a wrong fact, so you catch it before parents do.

What is AI answer accuracy for baby brands?

It's making sure that when AI describes your products, the facts are right: ingredients, age ranges, certifications, what they're free from. For baby and kids brands, where a wrong claim is a safety and trust issue, accuracy is the whole game. It's what Cowy specializes in.

How is Cowy different from an SEO agency?

SEO chases rankings. Cowy makes sure that when AI talks about your brand, it tells the truth. We find every wrong answer, fix the sources behind it, and prove the correction. We work only with baby and kids consumable brands, and we sell accuracy, not traffic.
