Restraints & challenges
HEADWIND
The commodity layer is being absorbed by platforms
Shopify auto-syndicates product data into ChatGPT, Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini, and infers missing attributes with ML. Free AI-visibility graders are multiplying. "Get listed in AI" is becoming a default feature, not a sellable service. This compresses the easy offer and forces the value up the stack.
HEADWIND
Measurement is hard; trust requires verification
There's no click data in agentic channels, so proving ROI takes server-side tracking and citation evidence. And 98% of AI-shoppers verify before buying (45% Google the brand, 78% say reviews drive trust). AI visibility alone doesn't convert; it must pair with a verification presence.
Future outlook (12-36 months)
• Visibility commoditizes; accuracy and recommendation-share appreciate. The defensible work moves to fact-correction, earned third-party authority, and winning head-to-head against named competitors.

• Agentic commerce shifts spend bottom-of-funnel. Product-data completeness, feed hygiene, schema, and checkout-API reliability matter more than blog content. New service line: "agent-readiness."

• Tooling consolidates. Profound hit a $1B valuation ($96M Series C, Feb 2026). Expect roll-ups. Niche, vertical-owned positions become acquisition targets.

INDIRECT · TOOL, NOT SERVICE
Profound, the 800lb gorilla
$1B valuation, $96M Series C (Feb 2026), fires millions of prompts daily across 10 engines. Has a Shopping Analysis module and new autonomous "Agents." But: enterprise-priced ($399/mo Growth, custom Enterprise), Starter is ChatGPT-only, no revenue attribution, "overkill for SMBs." It shows the problem; the founder still has to fix it.
INDIRECT · CHEAP TRACKERS
Otterly ($29+), Peec, Scrunch ($250+), Visiblie (€79)
Visibility-only trackers. They report mentions and share-of-voice but, in reviewers' words, "expect your team to architect solutions." Scrunch has an agency partner program (referral perks, client workspaces) you could resell on top of. These are your tooling layer, not your competition.
INDIRECT · PRODUCT-LEVEL
Ranketta, Goodie, Bluefish: ecommerce-leaning tools
Ranketta tracks individual products (not just brands) in AI shopping and ships copy-paste fixes; Shopify integration "in development." Goodie/Bluefish cover Amazon Rufus. Closest in spirit to Cowy's product focus, but still self-serve tools, still horizontal, no done-for-you delivery, no vertical.

Cowy's wedge
DEFENSIBLE
Done-for-you accuracy & recommendation-share, for one nervous vertical
Not "we monitor your AI visibility" (commoditizing). Instead: "we make sure AI names your baby brand over rivals and never states a wrong ingredient or safety fact, and we do the work so you don't touch code."
